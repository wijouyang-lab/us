## RELY — Remitly

**Decision: WATCH; confidence MEDIUM.**

Remitly is a digital cross-border remittance platform. Revenue comes from transaction fees and FX spread; growth is driven by active customers, send volume and corridor expansion. It is regulated as a money transmitter in many jurisdictions, so AML/KYC and licensing are core operating risks.

FY2025 revenue $1.635B, net income $67.9M, OCF $325.1M. TTM revenue $1.900B, net income $363.7M, OCF $409.4M, capex $14.9M, FCF $394.5M. The TTM P/E of 12.8 is misleading: the 2026 9M tax line includes a **$136M deferred-tax valuation-allowance release**, not recurring operating profit. Pre-tax income for 9M 2026 was $186.5M. SBC was **$155.1M** in FY2025, so FCF before SBC $395M falls to roughly $239M after SBC.

Valuation at $21.68: market cap $4.64B, net cash $669M, P/E normalized roughly high-20s, EV/FCF ~10 on reported FCF / ~16 after SBC. Scenario base $18.83, bear $12.47, bull $28.36. Current price is above base; no margin of safety.

Key risk: AML/regulatory action, price competition, SBC dilution. Catalyst: active-customer growth, take rate, compliance disclosures. Invalidation: regulatory penalty/license restriction or customer growth stall.

## FIVE — Five Below

**Decision: WATCH; confidence MEDIUM.**

Five Below is a discount retailer for teens/pre-teens with a treasure-hunt assortment, mostly third-party/imported goods. Revenue comes from comparable stores + new stores + e-commerce. Q2 FY2026 net sales $1.261B vs $1.027B (+22.8%); **comparable sales +14.1%** (transactions +13.6%, average ticket +0.4%). This is a genuine traffic-led comp, not just new stores.

However, the quarter includes a **$169.5M pre-tax IEEPA tariff refund** ($163.6M COGS reduction + $5.9M interest). Reported gross margin 48.5% vs 33.3%; excluding the refund, gross margin is roughly 35.6%. TTM net income $619.2M and FCF $599.3M include this one-time cash benefit. Normalized FCF is roughly $470M.

At $218.09, market cap $12.0B, net cash $561M. Reported P/E 19.4; normalized closer to mid-20s. Base scenario $163.86, bear $86.48, bull $229.98. Current price is above base; the strong comp does not create a margin of safety.

Key risk: refund reversal/one-time comp, consumer slowdown, inventory/tariffs, lease obligations. Catalyst: back-to-school/holiday comps and store productivity. Invalidation: negative comps or gross margin below pre-refund levels.

## USLM — United States Lime & Minerals

**Decision: WATCH; confidence MEDIUM.**

USLM produces lime and limestone for construction, steel, environmental and chemical customers, mainly in the southern/central U.S. Q3 2026 revenue $106.2M, net income $38.0M; **average selling price +2.5%, sales volume +1.6%**, with steel demand up and construction down. FY2025 revenue $372.7M, net income $134.3M, OCF $165.0M. TTM FCF $98.7M after $70.1M capex.

At $104.68, market cap $3.00B, net cash $439M; P/E 22.5, EV/FCF 26. Limestone reserves and regional quarries are real assets, but current margins are cyclical and capex is elevated. Base scenario $77.43, bear $44.59, bull $107.33. Current price is above base.

Key risk: construction/steel cycle, regional concentration, kiln/mine capex, resource depletion. Catalyst: price increases, volume recovery, capex normalization. Invalidation: price rollover or volume decline.

## FIZZ — National Beverage

**Decision: WATCH; confidence MEDIUM.**

National Beverage sells LaCroix, Power+ and other beverage brands. Q1 FY2027 net sales $330.7M, essentially flat; **average selling price +7.1%, case volume -6.4%**. Gross profit $115.8M, gross margin ~35.0% vs ~38.0% prior. Growth is price-driven, not volume-driven.

FY2026 revenue $1.181B, net income $183.6M, OCF $181.3M, capex $25.1M, FCF $161.9M. TTM P/E 19.9, EV/FCF 20.9, net cash $107M. SBC minimal ($0.4M). Base scenario $30.56, bear $17.81, bull $40.67. Current $37.25 is above base.

Key risk: volume erosion, brand competition, input/transport costs, family control. Catalyst: volume stabilization and LaCroix innovation. Invalidation: continued negative case volume or gross margin below 35%.
