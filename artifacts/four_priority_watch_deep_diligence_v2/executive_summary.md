## Key findings

1. **RELY P/E 12.8 is not real.** It includes a $136M deferred-tax valuation-allowance release; pre-tax 9M 2026 income was $186.5M. SBC $155M also reduces cash economics.
2. **FIVE comps are genuinely strong (+14.1%, transactions +13.6%)**, but reported gross margin 48.5% includes a **$169.5M tariff refund**. Normalized gross margin ~35.6% and normalized FCF ~$470M.
3. **USLM is a quality cyclical**, with Q3 price +2.5% and volume +1.6%; 22.5x P/E and 26x EV/FCF already price in solid conditions.
4. **FIZZ is price-driven, not volume-driven**: price +7.1%, volume -6.4%, gross margin down to ~35%. The brand is not growing units.
5. All four have net cash, but that does not compensate for one-time earnings, regulatory risk, or cyclicality.

## Which is most interesting?

- **Best business quality:** USLM (reserves/quarries, net cash) but cyclical and expensive.
- **Best operating momentum:** FIVE (traffic-led comp), but one-time refund and price above base.
- **Cheapest optically:** RELY, but the P/E is a tax artifact and SBC/regulatory risk are high.
- **Most likely value trap:** **RELY** — low headline P/E driven by a non-recurring tax release, with AML/regulatory risk; FIZZ also shows brand-volume erosion.

**No company is a PAPER candidate. Missing for PAPER:** normalized earnings/FCF across a full cycle, regulatory/legal diligence, defined margin of safety, and a catalyst path with price discipline.
