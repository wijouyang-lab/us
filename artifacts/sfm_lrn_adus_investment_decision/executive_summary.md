## What is actually good

- **ADUS**: organic same-store growth +6.9%; net cash; FCF $144M; low capex.
- **LRN**: TTM FCF $505M, net cash; P/E ~10. But Q1 OCF is -$125M and enrollment data is missing.
- **SFM**: 504 stores, +5% sales, net cash; but comps negative and FCF down $130M.

## What would change the decisions

- **SFM**: two consecutive quarters of positive comps, gross margin stabilization, capex normalization; price <$45.
- **LRN**: verifiable enrollment growth, state authorization renewals, Q1-to-Q4 cash conversion; price <$65.
- **ADUS**: Medicaid rate clarity, organic >6%, Homecourt integration; price <$100.

## Data gaps

- LRN student enrollment/retention and state concentration were not extractable as clean numeric series from the 10-Q/XBRL. This is a primary reason for LOW confidence.
- ADUS acquisition revenue contribution is not separately disclosed; organic is same-store growth, residual is UNKNOWN.
- SFM Q3 comps and gross margin are from the 10-Q; 8-quarter comps were not fully extracted.
- Quarterly OCF is YTD-tagged in SEC; `quarterly_financials.csv` marks this and does not pretend all quarters are standalone.
- No PIT, no adjusted total-return prices, no forward guidance model.

## Conclusion

**No PRIORITY_WATCH. No PAPER-50000 candidate. No trade.**
