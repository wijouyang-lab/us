# SFM — Sprouts Farmers Market

**Decision: WATCH** (not PRIORITY_WATCH)  
**Confidence: MEDIUM**  
**Price: $53.32 (2026-11-25); market cap $4.918B; net cash $104M**

## 1. Business

Sprouts operates 504 specialty grocery stores in 25 states (Q3 2026), focused on fresh, natural and organic food. Revenue comes from store sales; growth is primarily new stores, with comparable-store sales now negative. The customer is health-and-value-oriented; the model is a smaller-format specialty grocer, not a full-line supermarket.

## 2. Growth decomposition

Q3 2026:

- Net sales $2.305B, **+5%** year over year.
- Comparable-store sales **-0.9%**.
- 15 new stores opened; 504 total.
- YTD 2026 comps **-1.2%**, net sales **+5%**.

Therefore nearly all growth is **new-store growth**, not existing-store productivity. Q3 gross margin 38.2% vs 38.7% prior; management explicitly expects investments and lower comps to pressure margins near term. This is not a temporary one-quarter anomaly: comps are negative YTD.

## 3. Financials

TTM (FY2025 + 9M2026 − 9M2025):

- Revenue **$9.108B**
- Net income **$496.2M**
- OCF **$693.7M**
- Capex **$356.4M**
- FCF **$337.3M**

FY2025 comparison: revenue $8.806B, net income $523.7M, OCF $716.0M, capex $248.3M, FCF **$467.7M**. TTM FCF is down **$130M** because capex rose ~$108M and OCF fell ~$22M. The decline is mostly **higher capex**, not a one-quarter working-capital release. Q3 YTD OCF $555M and capex $284M confirm the heavier investment program.

Cash flow quality: positive, but FCF conversion is falling. The company remains net cash ($104M) and has no material solvency issue.

## 4. 8-quarter data

`quarterly_financials.csv` contains the 8-quarter revenue/net income series. OCF is tagged `YTD_*` where SEC only reports year-to-date cash flow; those values are **not** standalone quarterly OCF and are not used as such. The reliable cash-flow comparison is annual/TTM, not a fabricated 8-quarter FCF series.

## 5. Moat and competition

Strengths: differentiated fresh/organic assortment, small-box format, strong fresh distribution, 504-store base. Competitors: Kroger, Amazon/Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, regional grocers, mass merchants. The moat is moderate, not structural: assortment and store format can be copied, and the category is competitive.

## 6. Risks

- **Negative comps** while new stores drive sales: new-store productivity must carry the model.
- Gross margin pressure from investments, promotions, food costs and labor.
- Perishable inventory/shrink and new-store execution.
- Consumer discretionary/health-oriented spending.
- Valuation is not demanding (P/E 9.9, EV/FCF 14.3), but a low multiple on declining comps can be a value trap.

## 7. Valuation

TTM: P/E **9.9**, EV/FCF **14.3**, FCF yield **6.9%**, net cash.

Scenarios (FCF × multiple + net cash; not a target price):

| Scenario | FCF | Multiple | Implied equity | Implied price |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | $220M | 12x | $2.74B | **$29.75** |
| Base | $346M | 15x | $5.29B | **$57.39** |
| Bull | $420M | 18x | $7.66B | **$83.09** |

Current $53.32 is near the base case; the bear case implies ~44% downside. The stock is not expensive, but the current price does not compensate for negative comps and capex-driven FCF decline.

## 8. Catalysts / what to watch

- Q4 2026 comps and 2027 guidance.
- New-store productivity and capex normalization.
- Gross margin (38.2% Q3 2026).
- Inventory turns and shrink.
- Any return to positive comps.

**Watch price:** below ~$45 (roughly 8.5x TTM P/E, ~12x EV/FCF) **and** comps stabilizing. That is a watch trigger, not a buy order.

## 9. Verdict

Good operator, but current evidence shows **negative comps + rising capex + falling FCF**. WATCH. It is not the best risk/reward in the trio today, and it is not a PRIORITY_WATCH until comps turn.
