# ADUS Organic Growth Audit

Date: 2026-11-27

## Reported number

Q3 2026 company-reported **same-store revenue growth 6.9%**, versus 6.6% in Q3 2025. Total revenue grew:

```text
Q3 2026 $387.733M / Q3 2025 $362.301M - 1 = 7.02%
```

The residual after same-store growth is about **0.1pp**, but this is arithmetic,
not a company-disclosed acquisition contribution.

## Scope and definition

- Same-store growth is company-reported; the exact store/location inclusion
  criteria are in the 10-Q's operating data footnote, not independently
  recomputed by us.
- Homecourt acquisition closed **2026-05-01**.
- Q3 2026 includes Homecourt; Q3 2025 does not.
- The 10-Q does not separately disclose Homecourt revenue, so acquisition
  contribution cannot be independently isolated from the same-store base.
- Same-store growth is therefore **not** the same as total organic growth for
  the consolidated company; it excludes acquired locations and may exclude
  locations not open for the full comparison period.

## Conclusion

- Best evidence: **organic same-store +6.9%**.
- Acquisition/other contribution: **UNKNOWN** (cannot independently calculate;
  residual 0.1pp is not a disclosed number).
- Do not claim a precise organic/acquisition split.

This supports ADUS as the best organic grower of the three, but it does not
remove Medicaid rate, labor, or integration risk.
