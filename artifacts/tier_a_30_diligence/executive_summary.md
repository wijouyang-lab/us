## Recommended research order

1. **SFM** — cheapest quality retailer in the set; verify same-store sales and gross margin durability.
2. **LRN** — low P/E, strong FCF, but state-authorization risk needs legal/regulatory work.
3. **G** — recurring services, ~10% FCF yield; check AI cannibalization and organic growth.
4. **ADUS** — demographic tailwind and positive FCF; check payer rates and acquisition organic growth.
5. **TMDX** — real growth, positive FCF; wait for valuation or evidence of durable organ volumes.

## Eliminations

- **CRMD**: FY2025 looked profitable, but Q3 2026 revenue fell to $54.5M and net income was -$14.7M; reimbursement/contract concentration makes trailing P/E misleading.
- **GDYN**: ~117x P/E and ~47x EV/EBITDA with ~2.3% net margin; growth does not justify the multiple.
- **OSPN**: revenue flat at ~$243M for three years; FY2024-25 net income includes non-operating items, so ~12x P/E is not a clean operating multiple.

## Key findings

1. **Cheapness is concentrated in cyclical/value names** (SFM, GSL, LRN, G, DHI/PHM), not in the high-quality compounders (CHD, WDFC, PPG, IPAR), which remain expensive.
2. **Cash flow quality separates the list**: G, LRN, SFM, ADUS, COLL and GSL generate FCF well above net income; GDYN and FLXS do not.
3. **Several apparent bargains are accounting traps**: CRMD's FY2025 tax/operating swing, OSPN's non-operating gain, GSL's peak-cycle shipping earnings.
4. **Leverage is a real constraint** for PRG (~3.3x), HII (~2.3x), PPG (~1.4x) and AMTM; it is low for ADUS, G, LRN, SFM and TMDX.
5. **Data is current cross-section, not PIT**: prices are latest yfinance closes; fundamentals are SEC XBRL companyfacts. Some XBRL tags and share counts are incomplete; NRDS market cap uses FS2 fallback.
